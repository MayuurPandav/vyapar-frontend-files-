⚠️ The Everyday Struggle

Why Indian Merchants Struggle Today

Traditional paper books and legacy desktop tools create friction and loss every single day.

📒

Messy Paper Khata

Customer credit entries get lost, forgotten, or disputed. Manual phone calls for payment collection feel awkward.

📦

Blind Inventory

Popular items run out of stock without notice, while slow-moving items expire unnoticed in warehouses.

⏳

Slow Counter Queues

Typing prices by hand creates long queues, manual calculation mistakes, and missed sales during peak rush.

🚚

Zero Delivery Visibility

Customers constantly call asking "Where is my parcel?" Merchants have no idea where drivers are.

💡 The Modern Solution

Introducing Vyapar 2.0

A unified, web-first platform designed to run an entire business from a single screen.

⚡

Instant GST Billing

Generate professional A4 Tax Invoices or instant 58mm/80mm counter POS thermal receipts with automated tax compliance.

🏷️

Barcode Scan Engine

Automatically generate standard EAN-13 barcodes and QR codes. Print full A4 barcode sticker sheets in one click.

💳

Digital Ledger Khata

Track real-time customer balances. Send automated WhatsApp payment reminders with direct online payment links.

👥

Multi-Role Portals

Clean, dedicated portals for Store Owners, Cashier POS counters, Delivery Partners, and Accountants.

📊 Core Architecture

Key Operational Modules

Four integrated pillars powering the entire business workflow in real-time.

🧾

Sales & Invoicing

Create customized invoices with dynamic line discounts, multi-tier GST slabs, and instant PDF downloads in 1 click.

🛒

Purchase & Suppliers

Record supplier stock-in shipments, track accounts payable, and manage vendor payment histories with zero errors.

💰

Expense Ledger

Categorize daily shop expenses (rent, salaries, electricity) to get an accurate daily net profit calculation.

🤝

Parties & Khata CRM

Complete 360-degree party profile with detailed transaction ledgers, credit limits, and settlement tracking.

🌟 The Showstopper Feature

Real-Time GPS Delivery Tracking

The competitive edge that traditional software like Tally or Marg completely lack.

🔗 Public Link — Zero Login Required

Customers receive an SMS or WhatsApp link to view their live delivery tracking directly in their mobile browser.

🗺️ Live GPS Telematics & Interactive Map

Live moving driver marker powered by Leaflet, displaying actual route geometry and vehicle coordinates.

⏱️ Dynamic Real-Time ETA

Calculates remaining distance in kilometers and updates estimated delivery time in real time as the driver travels.

🛠️ Architecture & Tech Stack

Built on Rock-Solid Modern Tech

Designed for sub-second response times, zero server crashes, and enterprise scalability.

⚛️

React 19 + Vite

Blazing-fast client bundle, instant route transitions, zero page reloads, and mobile responsiveness.

🟢

Node.js & Express

High-throughput RESTful architecture powering live GPS streams, PDFKit generators, and barcode rendering.

🍃

MongoDB Atlas

Cloud-native NoSQL database handling dynamic catalog structures and geospatial delivery points safely.

🔒

Enterprise Security

Strict JWT role-based access control (Admin, Driver, Cashier), encrypted credentials, and GSTR-1 audit exports.
